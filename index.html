About me

From monsoon variability
to the global carbon cycle.

I am an atmospheric scientist working at the intersection of global greenhouse-gas transport, observation-constrained inverse modelling, and climate–carbon-cycle variability.

At the National Institute for Environmental Studies, I combine satellite and in-situ CO₂ observations with NICAM-TM and 4D-Var inversion systems to investigate greenhouse-gas sources and sinks and the variability of terrestrial carbon uptake. My earlier work examined Indian summer monsoon processes using regional climate models.

PhD, Atmospheric Modelling · IIT Kharagpur, 2018
MSc, Applied Mathematics · BSc (Honours), Mathematics · Vidyasagar University

Research interests

Three connected perspectives.

Understanding variability through the interplay of observations, physical models, and statistical inference.

01 / CARBON

Greenhouse gases & the carbon cycle

Estimating CO₂ sources and sinks and investigating how climate variability influences terrestrial carbon uptake.

CO₂ / CH₄ · Surface fluxes · Carbon–climate interactions
02 / METHODS

Atmospheric modelling & data assimilation

Combining transport simulations with satellite and in-situ observations to constrain greenhouse-gas fluxes.

Inverse modelling · 4D-Var · Ensemble methods
03 / CLIMATE

Monsoon & land–atmosphere interactions

Exploring Indian summer monsoon variability, ENSO relationships, and the role of land-surface processes.

Regional climate · ENSO · Soil moisture
